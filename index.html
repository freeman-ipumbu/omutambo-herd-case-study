ONE PLACE FOR THE DAILY WORK

The detail matters.
So does the bigger picture.

Keep useful records close, follow up on the work that needs doing, and see operational measures built only from what you enter.

01

Manage every livestock type.

Keep cattle, goats, sheep and freely named animal types in one tagged register with photographs, breed, class, camp, status, health and breeding context.

02

Run poultry with evidence.

Manage flocks and housing, then record eggs, feed, mortality, weights, health, cleaning, water and biosecurity. Review lay rate, feed intensity and capacity pressure without invented data.

03

Connect work and recovery.

Track camps, movements, tasks and NAD costs. Export separate registers, keep photo-inclusive backups and review versioned cloud revisions.

ACCESS BY INVITATION

A public front door.
A private workspace.

You need an invitation from the owner to enter. Each invited tester receives a separate demonstration workspace; the owner's live farm stays private.

Sign in or activate ↗
  1. 01

    Receive your invitation

    The owner gives you a one-time activation code, linked to your email address.

  2. 02

    Verify your email

    Sign in through Cloudflare using the email on your invitation. Enter the email verification PIN there.

  3. 03

    Activate and get to work

    Enter your invitation code once. Your permission and expiry are set by the owner. Returning users can open their workspace after signing in.
